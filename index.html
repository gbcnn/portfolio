About

I'm Gabi Canena, a Graphic Designer, Illustrator and Art Director based in Brazil, working globally since 2018. I build brands, campaigns, and visual systems that stick. My work lives at the intersection of design and illustration: from brand identities and key visuals to large-scale event design and custom illustration for digital products. I've worked with Ubisoft, Amazon, Acer, SanDisk, AMD, FURIA, and more. I'm driven by the combination of sensitivity, intuition, and craft. Currently open to global remote projects in design, illustration, and branding.
